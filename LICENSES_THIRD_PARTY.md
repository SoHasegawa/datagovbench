# Third-Party Dataset Licenses

This benchmark aggregates publicly available datasets from open-data portals.
Each dataset retains its original license; this file enumerates them.
The benchmark code and packaging are licensed separately (see LICENSE).

> **Important — NonCommercial.** Because this benchmark includes one or more
> datasets under a NonCommercial license (e.g. CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0), the benchmark
> as a whole may only be used for non-commercial purposes. Commercial use of
> the benchmark — including evaluation of commercial products — is prohibited
> unless those datasets are removed from your copy.
